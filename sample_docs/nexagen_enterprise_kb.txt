1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

NexaGen Technologies Inc. was founded in 2018 by Dr. Priya Sharma and Marcus Chen in Austin, Texas. The company specializes in enterprise AI solutions for the healthcare, finance, and logistics industries. As of Q3 2026, NexaGen employs 2,847 people across 12 offices worldwide, with annual revenue of $485 million.

The company's mission is: "To democratize enterprise AI by making intelligent automation accessible, explainable, and trustworthy for every organization."

NexaGen is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol NXGN. The current CEO is Dr. Priya Sharma, CTO is James Rodriguez, and CFO is Sarah Kim.

2. PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

2.1 NexaGen Cortex (Flagship Product)
NexaGen Cortex is the company's flagship enterprise AI platform. It provides:
- Document Intelligence: Automated extraction, classification, and summarization of enterprise documents including contracts, invoices, medical records, and regulatory filings.
- Predictive Analytics: Machine learning models for demand forecasting, risk assessment, and anomaly detection.
- Workflow Automation: Low-code AI-powered workflow builder with 200+ pre-built connectors for SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, and Microsoft 365.
- Compliance Engine: Automated regulatory compliance checking against SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI-DSS standards.

Pricing: Starting at $15,000/month for up to 500 users. Enterprise tier at $45,000/month for unlimited users with dedicated support.

Current customers: 340+ enterprise clients including 42 Fortune 500 companies.
Key clients: BlueCross BlueShield, JPMorgan Chase, FedEx, Mayo Clinic, and Siemens.

2.2 NexaGuard (Security Product)
NexaGuard is the company's AI-powered cybersecurity platform launched in March 2024. Features:
- Real-time threat detection using behavioral analysis and anomaly detection.
- Automated incident response with playbook-based remediation.
- Vulnerability scanning across cloud, on-premise, and hybrid environments.
- Zero-trust network architecture enforcement.
- Integration with CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, and Splunk.

NexaGuard processes an average of 2.3 billion security events per day across all customers. The platform has a mean detection time (MDT) of 4.2 seconds and mean response time (MRT) of 12 seconds.

Pricing: $8,500/month for standard tier. $22,000/month for enterprise tier with 24/7 SOC support.

2.3 NexaHealth (Healthcare-Specific Solution)
NexaHealth is a specialized platform for healthcare providers, launched in January 2025. Features:
- Clinical document understanding and ICD-10/CPT code suggestion.
- Patient risk stratification using EHR data integration.
- Drug interaction checking across 50,000+ known interactions.
- HIPAA-compliant data processing with end-to-end encryption.
- Integration with Epic, Cerner, and Meditech EHR systems.

Current deployments: 78 hospitals and 230 clinics across the United States.
Notable results: 34% reduction in administrative burden, 22% improvement in coding accuracy, and $4.2 million average annual savings per hospital.

3. ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY

3.1 Technology Stack
- Backend: Python (FastAPI, Django), Go (microservices), Rust (performance-critical components)
- Frontend: React, Next.js, TypeScript
- AI/ML: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face Transformers, custom foundation models
- Infrastructure: AWS (primary), Google Cloud (secondary), Kubernetes (EKS), Terraform
- Databases: PostgreSQL (transactional), MongoDB (document store), Redis (caching), Qdrant (vector search)
- Message Queue: Apache Kafka (event streaming), RabbitMQ (task queues)
- Monitoring: Datadog, PagerDuty, Grafana

3.2 AI Models
NexaGen develops and maintains several proprietary AI models:

- NexaLM-7B: A 7-billion parameter language model fine-tuned for enterprise document understanding. Trained on 2.1 trillion tokens of enterprise-specific data. Achieves 94.2% accuracy on the DocVQA benchmark.

- NexaLM-70B: The larger variant used for complex reasoning tasks. Available only in the Enterprise tier. Requires GPU inference (NVIDIA A100 or H100).

- NexaVision-3B: A multimodal model for document image understanding, table extraction, and handwriting recognition. Processes 15 pages per second on A100 GPUs.

- NexaCode-13B: A code generation model specialized for enterprise integration scripts, SQL queries, and API connector development.

All models are hosted on NexaGen's private cloud infrastructure. No customer data is sent to third-party AI providers. Models are retrained quarterly with the latest data.

3.3 Data Security Architecture
- All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- Customer data is isolated in dedicated tenants (single-tenant architecture for Enterprise tier).
- SOC 2 Type II certified since 2021. ISO 27001 certified since 2023.
- Annual penetration testing by Mandiant. Zero critical vulnerabilities in the last 3 years.
- Data retention: Customer data retained for 90 days after contract termination, then permanently deleted.
- Backup: Daily incremental backups, weekly full backups. Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 1 hour. Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours.

4. HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES

4.1 Leave Policy
- Paid Time Off (PTO): 25 days per year for all full-time employees.
- Sick Leave: 12 days per year. Unused sick leave carries over up to 30 days.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers, 8 weeks for secondary caregivers.
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family.
- Sabbatical: Employees with 5+ years of tenure are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical.

4.2 Remote Work Policy
NexaGen operates on a hybrid work model effective since January 2024:
- Engineering teams: 2 days in office (Tuesday and Thursday), 3 days remote.
- Sales and marketing: 3 days in office, 2 days remote.
- Executive leadership: 4 days in office.
- Fully remote positions available for roles that don't require physical presence, subject to manager approval.
- Home office stipend: $1,500 one-time setup allowance + $75/month internet reimbursement.

4.3 Compensation & Benefits
- Annual salary review in March. Average raise: 4-6% based on performance.
- Stock options: Vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
- 401(k) matching: Company matches 100% up to 6% of salary.
- Health insurance: Fully covered for employees. 80% covered for dependents. Provider: Aetna PPO.
- Dental and vision: Fully covered for employees and dependents. Provider: Delta Dental and VSP.
- Professional development budget: $3,000 per year per employee for conferences, courses, and certifications.
- Employee referral bonus: $5,000 for engineering roles, $3,000 for non-engineering roles.

4.4 Performance Review Process
NexaGen uses a quarterly performance review cycle:
- Q1 (January): Goal setting and OKR alignment.
- Q2 (April): Mid-year check-in with manager feedback.
- Q3 (July): Self-assessment and peer review (360-degree feedback).
- Q4 (October): Final review, calibration, and compensation adjustment.
Performance ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement.
Employees with "Needs Improvement" are placed on a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

6.2 Service Level Agreements
- Platform Uptime: 99.95% guaranteed (allows ~4.4 hours downtime per year).
- Actual uptime in 2025: 99.98%.
- Scheduled maintenance windows: Sunday 2AM-6AM EST (announced 7 days in advance).
- Incident severity levels:
  - P1 (Critical): Complete service outage. Response: 15 minutes. Resolution target: 2 hours.
  - P2 (High): Major feature degraded. Response: 1 hour. Resolution target: 8 hours.
  - P3 (Medium): Minor feature issue. Response: 4 hours. Resolution target: 48 hours.
  - P4 (Low): Cosmetic or documentation issue. Response: 24 hours. Resolution target: 5 business days.

6.3 SLA Credits
If NexaGen fails to meet the uptime SLA:
- 99.9% to 99.95%: 10% credit on monthly bill.
- 99.0% to 99.9%: 25% credit on monthly bill.
- Below 99.0%: 50% credit on monthly bill.
Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident.
